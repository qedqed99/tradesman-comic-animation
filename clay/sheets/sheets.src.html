Clay model sheets for Noah, Lu and the Boss

Drafted from your comic, not from the v1 cartoon puppets. Each sheet pins down what has to stay true to the comic (silhouette, hair, face, outfit, colors) so the clay builds match it. Correct anything that is wrong. The handwritten questions are the things I could not tell from the comic.

Correct in plain words

Reply in the thread, e.g. "Lu's glasses are thinner and more round" or "Boss wears jeans". Short notes are enough.

Or mark it up

Screenshot a sheet, scribble on it on your phone, and drop the image in the thread. Rough is fine.

Then a clay turnaround

Once the sheets are right, I sculpt each character in Blender and send front, ¾, side and back views plus three expressions for approval before any scene is rendered.

Noah

The kid with the clipboard and the red scarfSHEET 1 / 3

What must read

Silhouette
Small, slim kid, about chest-height to Lu. The scarf tails stream out behind him whenever he moves.
Head
Round face, soft chin, small button nose, big brown eyes with white highlights, very expressive brows.
Hair
Medium brown, short and shaggy. Pointed tufts fall over the forehead, with a few strands sticking up at the crown.
Outfit
Slate-blue crew-neck tee, olive-khaki shorts to the knee, white crew socks, brown lace-up boots.
Signature
Red scarf knotted at the throat with two long tails (worn like a cape). Clipboard and pen, and a phone in the call scene.
Acting range
Focused, startled, panicked yell, beaming grin, wide-eyed awe.

Clay translation

  • Head about a third of his height, so he reads as a kid next to the adults.
  • Hair sculpted as a few big swooping clay locks, not a pile of balls.
  • Scarf as a thin rolled sheet with sculpted folds, so it can flap.
  • Sculpted eyelids and brows instead of v1's plain bead eyes.

Questions for you

  1. Roughly how old is Noah meant to look?
  2. Scarf or a real cape? The comic draws a scarf with long tails.
  3. Anything about the real Noah the comic left out?

Lu

The helper who is always, relentlessly helpfulSHEET 2 / 3

What must read

Silhouette
Tall and slim, long legs, narrow shoulders. The lightest-built of the three.
Head
Oval face with a defined jaw, clean-shaven or the faintest shadow. A huge open-mouth smile showing teeth.
Hair
Black, short at the sides, longer on top and swept up and back, a little messy.
Glasses
Thin wire frames in amber-brown, softly rounded rectangles.
Outfit
Cream tee with the Olympic rings on the chest (blue, black, red over yellow, green), black slim trousers, light grey sneakers.
Acting range
Beaming wave, determined chase, flat grumpy "No!", laughing at the BBQ.

The Boss

Laid-back, hot-tub-dwelling, comes to the rescue in the brown sedanSHEET 3 / 3

What must read

Silhouette
Broad and stocky with thick arms and a solid chest. Wider than Lu, a little shorter.
Head
Always a black rolled-cuff beanie and black sunglasses. A short full beard, dark brown with grey flecks. A big crinkly grin.
Outfit
Sage or army-green crew tee. The comic never shows his legs except in the tub (dark swim shorts).
Signature
Phone to his ear in the tub. Drives the brown four-door sedan.
Acting range
Big laugh, confident "I'll take care of it", surprised O-mouth in the car.

Clay translation

  • Shades as one glossy black piece, so his eyes never need animating; the brows and grin do the acting.
  • Beard textured with tool marks, to set it apart from the smooth skin.
  • The beanie's rolled cuff as a separate sculpted band.

Questions for you

  1. What does he wear below the waist: jeans, shorts, work pants?
  2. How grey is the beard in real life?
  3. Does the shirtless hot-tub version need any tattoos or other details?

Line-up and scale

In the comic Noah's head reaches about Lu's chest. The Boss is the widest, and Lu is the tallest and thinnest. The clay builds will keep those ratios so the trio reads at a glance even in wide shots.

What would help most

Short answers to the handwritten questions, and anything about the real people the comic didn't capture. If you still have the separate character images from making the comic, those are the best reference of all, but they are optional.
